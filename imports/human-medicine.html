Human Medicine

Licensed by the Ethiopian Food and Drug Authority as an importer and wholesale distributor supplying hospitals, pharmacies and health programmes across Ethiopia with medicines from WHO-GMP-certified manufacturers.

Specification
LicenceEFDA-licensed import and wholesale
ManufacturersOver 100, all WHO-GMP-certified
Therapeutic rangeAnti-infectives to oncology, vaccines, OTC
Delivery24h Addis Ababa; 48-72h regional centres
Cold storageBonded, at Addis Ababa warehouse

Regulatory pathway

Every medicine comes from an EFDA-approved source, with pre-import permits and product registrations secured before shipment. The team handles the full compliance lifecycle: CTD-format dossiers, product registration and variation management, and post-market pharmacovigilance against national and international safety standards.

Sourcing and quality

Supply comes only from manufacturers holding current WHO Good Manufacturing Practice certification. Every consignment carries a batch-analysis certificate, with random third-party laboratory verification and continuous stability monitoring where required. Therapeutic classes span anti-infectives, cardiovascular agents, anti-diabetics, oncology therapies, antiretrovirals, vaccines and OTC products.

Cold chain

For vaccines, biologics and insulin, a validated cold chain runs from manufacturer to final delivery: active and passive temperature-controlled packaging, real-time data loggers giving a complete audit trail, bonded cold storage at the Addis Ababa warehouse, and specialist handling for Category A and B products.

Nationwide distribution

Central warehousing and partner logistics providers serve registered hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and government health programmes. Delivery runs within 24 hours in Addis Ababa and 48-72 hours to major regional centres, in volumes from a single carton to full-container loads.
