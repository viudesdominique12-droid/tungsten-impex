What it is

Calcium hypochlorite is the main active ingredient of commercial bleaching powder. It sanitises public swimming pools, disinfects drinking water, and is used in water treatment and as a bleaching agent.

Sourcing and quality

The material is sourced from international manufacturers. Incoming consignments are checked against the required specifications for purity, chemical composition and consistency.
