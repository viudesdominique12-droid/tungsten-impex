Electric Vehicles

Tungsten imports the BYD Yuan Up, a battery electric SUV offered in three trims with 301 to 401 km of combined range.

The vehicle

A battery electric SUV launched on 26 March 2024, sold in three trims: 301 km Lingxian, 401 km Lingxian and 401 km Chaoyue. Five seats; 4,310 by 1,830 by 1,675 mm, on a 2,620 mm wheelbase.

Battery and charging

Every trim uses BYD's Blade Battery, 32 kWh on the 301 km trim and 45.12 kWh on the two 401 km trims. A 400-volt architecture supports AC and DC fast charging, taking the battery from 30 to 80 per cent in 30 minutes.

Performance

An AC permanent magnet synchronous motor rated 70 kW and 180 Nm, or 130 kW and 290 Nm on the 401 km trims. Acceleration from 0 to 100 km/h takes 12 seconds, or 7.9 seconds on the higher-powered trims.

The manufacturer

BYD works across electronics, automotives, renewable energy and rail transit, with more than 30 industrial parks across six continents and a focus on energy generation and storage. Vehicles are imported through Tungsten.
