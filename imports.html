What we import

Everything below is brought into Ethiopia by us, cleared by us and delivered by us.

Electric Vehicles

Tungsten imports the BYD Yuan Up, a battery electric SUV offered in three trims with 301 to 401 km of combined range.

Medical Equipment

An EFDA-licensed importer that supplies, installs and maintains hospital equipment across Ethiopia, with a dedicated regulatory team handling clearance and in-house biomedical engineers handling commissioning and long-term service.

Human Medicine

Licensed by the Ethiopian Food and Drug Authority as an importer and wholesale distributor supplying hospitals, pharmacies and health programmes across Ethiopia with medicines from WHO-GMP-certified manufacturers.

Building Glass

Tungsten supplies building and housing glass — float, sheet, figured, mirror, safety and specialty — and the machines to cut, temper and finish it, from one import source.

Elevator & Escalator

Supply, installation, modernisation and maintenance of passenger lifts for residential and commercial buildings, with safety systems said to meet industry standards and regulations.

Calcium Hypochlorite

Tungsten Import Export is currently importing calcium hypochlorite, the active ingredient in commercial bleaching powder, used for water treatment, drinking-water disinfection and swimming-pool sanitation.

Plastic Raw Materials

Tungsten Import Export imports plastic raw materials and master batch, and is looking for recycled and virgin HDPE from a reliable long-term source.

Solar Lanterns

Tungsten imports and distributes quality-approved units for off-grid rural households, replacing kerosene lamps and candles with light that also charges phones and runs small electronics.

Stationery Materials

Tungsten imports stationery across writing, paper, filing and desk supplies, letting a buyer source a full home and office order from one importer.

Ceramics

Imported ceramic products, sanitary ware, bath-related products and finishing materials, with importing capacity being increased to keep pace with demand from the marketplace.
