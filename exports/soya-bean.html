Soya Bean

A legume native to East Asia, grown for its edible bean and processed into soy milk, tofu and fermented foods — one of four Tungsten export lines.

Specification
Crop typeLegume, grown for edible bean
OriginNative to East Asia
Unfermented usesSoy milk, tofu, tofu skin
Fermented usesSoy sauce, bean paste, nattō, tempeh
Export rangeOne of four export crops
Export experienceMore than 20 years

From bean to product

The unfermented route runs through soy milk, from which tofu and tofu skin are made. Fermented soya foods include soy sauce, fermented bean paste, nattō and tempeh.

Where it sits

Tungsten Import Export offers soya bean alongside sesame seed, niger seed (noug) and red kidney beans. The company states more than 20 years of export experience.
