What we export

Ethiopian oilseeds and pulses, sourced and shipped with three decades of experience behind them.

Niger Seed (Noug)

Pressed in Ethiopia for edible cooking oil, this oilseed passed coffee among the country's US exports in 2002 and now accounts for a third of them.

Sesame Seed

Buyers choose between three well-known Ethiopian sesame varieties: Humera and Gonder for bakery products, Wellega for its high oil content.

Red Kidney Beans

Grown in Ethiopia and known locally as Qey Boloqe, these red kidney beans are popular across Scandinavia, Germany and wider Europe, and in India and Pakistan.

Soya Bean

A legume native to East Asia, grown for its edible bean and processed into soy milk, tofu and fermented foods — one of four Tungsten export lines.
